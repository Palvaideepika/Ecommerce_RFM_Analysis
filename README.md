# E-Commerce Customer Segmentation Using RFM Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce customer purchasing behavior using **RFM Analysis (Recency, Frequency, and Monetary Value)** and **K-Means Clustering**. The objective is to identify different customer groups and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention, engagement, and revenue.

## Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Segment customers using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary metrics.
- Identify valuable, loyal, and at-risk customers.
- Apply K-Means clustering to discover customer groups.
- Create an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize insights.
- Recommend targeted marketing strategies for each customer segment.

## Dataset

The project uses the Online Retail dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository.

The dataset contains transaction-level information, including invoice numbers, product details, quantities, invoice dates, prices, customer IDs, and countries.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Load the Online Retail dataset.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Remove records with missing customer IDs, canceled invoices, non-positive quantities, and non-positive unit prices.
3. **Feature Engineering:** Calculate revenue as Quantity × Unit Price.
4. **RFM Analysis:**
   - **Recency:** Days since the customer's most recent purchase.
   - **Frequency:** Number of distinct invoices per customer.
   - **Monetary:** Total revenue generated by each customer.
5. **Customer Segmentation:** Calculate RFM scores and classify customers into business segments.
6. **K-Means Clustering:** Apply clustering to scaled RFM features to identify customer groups.
7. **Visualization:** Create charts to explore customer segments, clusters, and RFM patterns.
8. **Dashboard Development:** Build a Power BI dashboard with customer metrics, charts, and slicers.
9. **Business Recommendations:** Suggest targeted strategies for each customer segment.

## Customer Segments

- **Champions:** Recent, frequent customers with high spending.
- **Loyal Customers:** Customers who purchase frequently.
- **Recent Customers:** Customers who have purchased recently but have lower purchase frequency.
- **Potential Loyalists:** Customers who may become repeat buyers.
- **At Risk:** Previously engaged customers who may need re-engagement.
- **Lost / Hibernating Customers:** Customers with low recent engagement and lower purchase frequency.

*Note: Segment labels are determined by the project's RFM scoring rules.*

## K-Means Clustering

K-Means clustering is used to group customers based on their Recency, Frequency, and Monetary values. Log transformation and feature scaling help reduce skew and bring the metrics onto comparable scales.

The model uses four clusters to explore customer purchasing patterns.

## Business Recommendations

- Reward Champions with loyalty benefits and exclusive offers.
- Encourage Loyal Customers to continue purchasing through personalized rewards.
- Offer new-customer promotions to increase second purchases.
- Engage Potential Loyalists with relevant recommendations.
- Run win-back campaigns for At Risk customers.
- Evaluate low-cost reactivation offers for Lost / Hibernating customers.
